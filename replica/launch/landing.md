# Landing page (internal): /welcome

Audience: everyone at the company, on the day the new service desk opens. Built at `src/app/welcome/page.tsx`, linked from sign-in.

1. Hero: "Ask for help, and always see where it's at." Under it: "Glasswork is our new IT help desk. You'll know who has your request, when to expect a reply, and what it's waiting on." Button: Sign in. (Screenshot: the real request page, replica/clone-screens/S05.png.)
2. The problem (paraphrased research, no quotes): you raise something and hear nothing; you can't tell who has it or when it'll be done; too many emails about the wrong things.
3. How it works: pick what you need → describe it → follow it on one page.
4. Features, fixes first: what to expect (reply and fix times in plain words); who's on it and related work; choose your emails; then the basics (approvals, help articles).
5. Pricing: not applicable (internal; no per-seat cost).
6. FAQ: Do I need a new password? Can I still email IT? (not yet; use the help center). Will my old tickets move over? (no; open ones are re-raised by IT). Who sees what I write? (the IT team; internal notes are never shown to you, and you never see theirs).
7. Final call to action: Sign in.

No testimonials, user counts or logos. Contrast: uses the checked tokens only.
